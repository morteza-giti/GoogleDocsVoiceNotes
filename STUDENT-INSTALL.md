# Listening to your teacher's voice notes

Your teacher leaves spoken feedback as comments in your Google Docs. You can
always listen by clicking the link in the comment. Installing this small
extension adds a **play button right inside the comment**, so it is quicker.

It works in **Google Chrome** and **Microsoft Edge** on a computer. It does not
work on phones.

## Install (about 3 minutes)

1. Download the file `voice-notes-extension.zip` your teacher sent you.
2. Right-click it and choose **Extract All** (Windows) or double-click it (Mac).
   Put the folder somewhere you will not delete, such as your Documents folder.
   **Do not delete or move this folder later**, or the extension will stop working.
3. Open your browser's extensions page:
   - Chrome: type `chrome://extensions` in the address bar and press Enter.
   - Edge: type `edge://extensions` in the address bar and press Enter.
4. Turn on **Developer mode** (a switch on the page).
5. Click **Load unpacked**, then choose the folder from step 2 (the one that
   contains a file called `manifest.json`).
6. You should now see **Google Docs Voice Notes** in your list.

## Use it

1. Open the Google Doc your teacher shared with you and refresh the page.
2. Click a comment that has a voice note. A player appears under the comment.
3. Press the play button.

The first time, a Google window asks you to sign in:
1. Choose your Google account.
2. If it says **"Google hasn't verified this app"**, this is normal. Click
   **Advanced**, then **Go to Voice Notes (unsafe)**, then **Allow**.

The extension only plays audio files from your teacher's folder. It never
changes or deletes anything of yours.

## If something does not work

- **No play button:** refresh the Google Doc page. Make sure the extension is
  switched on in the extensions page.
- **"Access blocked" when signing in:** tell your teacher, who needs to add
  your Google email address first.
- **Nothing helps:** click the link in the comment instead. It opens the
  audio in Google Drive, where you can play it.
